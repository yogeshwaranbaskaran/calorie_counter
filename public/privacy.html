Privacy

Calorie Counter · last updated 21 September 2026

Calorie Counter is a small food and weight tracker run by one person for themselves and a few friends. It is not a company, it has no analytics, no advertising, and nothing is sold or shared.

What is stored

  • Your Google account's email address, display name and profile picture, used only to sign you in and show who is signed in.
  • What you log: the foods you eat, their quantities and nutrition, your weight readings, and any targets you set.
  • Optionally, a Gemini API key you choose to provide. It is encrypted before it is stored and is never shown back to you or anyone else.

Who can see it

Only you. Every record is tied to your account and served only to a signed-in session for that account. The person running the service can technically access the database, and does not look at other people's logs.

Where it goes

When you log a meal, the text you typed is sent to Google's Gemini API to work out its nutrition, using either your own key or the app's. Google's handling of that request is governed by Google's Gemini API terms. Nothing else leaves the service. Data is stored in a Postgres database hosted by Neon and the app runs on Render.

Cookies

One cookie, to keep you signed in for 30 days. No tracking cookies.

Deleting your data

Any entry, weight reading or food can be removed inside the app. To delete your account and everything with it, email the address below and it will be removed from the database.
